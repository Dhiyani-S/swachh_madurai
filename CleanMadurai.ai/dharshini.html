🌿 CleanMadurai.AI — About & Impact

Problem Statement · Innovation · SDG Goals

📌 Problem Statement

Madurai, one of Tamil Nadu's oldest and most culturally significant cities, is home to over 1.5 million people across 5 administrative zones and 100 wards. Every single day, the city generates more than 650 tonnes of solid waste from residential areas, commercial establishments, public spaces, and sanitation facilities. Despite the dedication of the Corporation of Madurai's sanitation workforce, the city continues to struggle with an increasingly complex waste management crisis that threatens public health, environmental sustainability, and the dignity of its citizens.


The core problem is not a lack of workers or resources — it is the complete absence of intelligent, data-driven systems to manage, monitor, and predict waste generation across the city. Hotels, meat shops, chicken stalls, hostels, and commercial markets generate disproportionately large volumes of waste daily, yet there is no structured on-demand pickup system. Shop owners resort to dumping waste on streets, creating severe hygiene hazards and breeding grounds for disease vectors including mosquitoes and rats.


Public street dustbins overflow regularly because there is no real-time monitoring to alert sanitation workers before bins reach capacity. Critically, there is no separation of biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and sanitary waste at the source, making downstream processing nearly impossible. Sanitary napkin disposal remains one of the most neglected waste management challenges in urban India. The sanitation workforce — 900+ dedicated workers — operates without incentives, performance tracking, or digital tools. At the governance level, Corporation Commissioners operate without real-time data or predictive analytics, making decisions based on historical habit rather than current intelligence.

💡 Solution Summary

CleanMadurai.AI is an end-to-end AI-powered urban waste management and sanitation intelligence platform built specifically for the Corporation of Madurai. Three trained ML models built on real Madurai Corporation data predict bin overflow with 91% accuracy, forecast daily waste volume per ward with ±98kg precision, and classify worker performance with 95% accuracy — enabling proactive decision-making instead of reactive crisis management. Commercial waste generators request pickups digitally, workers receive instant task assignments, QR-based verification ensures accountability at every step, and automated PDF receipts complete the payment cycle.

🚀 Innovation Highlights

🤖 Predictive Intelligence over Reactive Management

Traditional waste management is entirely reactive. CleanMadurai.AI introduces three purpose-trained ML models: Bin Overflow Predictor (91.35% accuracy), Waste Volume Forecaster (±98kg MAE), and Worker Performance Classifier (95.45% accuracy) — all trained on real Madurai Corporation data.

📱 QR-Based Worker Accountability System

Each worker gets a unique QR identity card for task completion proof, disposal center entry, attendance marking, and performance records — replacing paper-based reporting with tamper-proof digital credentials.

⭐ Incentive-Driven Behavioral Change

The Green Points system translates every completed task into salary increment — a dignity-first incentive architecture where worker income growth and city cleanliness improvement become the same goal.

🔄 Complete Digital Waste Lifecycle

Every piece of waste is tracked from shop owner request → worker assignment → QR collection scan → zone disposal center → compost/recycling output — creating full end-to-end traceability.
